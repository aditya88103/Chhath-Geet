🕉️ About Chhath Tithi

Chhath Puja is observed on the Shashti (6th) Tithi of Shukla Paksha in the month of Kartik (October–November). The festival spans 4 days starting from Panchami. Times may vary by 5–15 minutes depending on local geography. Always consult a local panchang for exact muhurat.
