History & Origins

ऋग्वैदिक काल से चली आ रही सूर्य आराधना

Chhath Puja is one of the oldest surviving festivals in the world, with roots extending directly into the Rigveda, where hymns worshipping the Sun God (Surya) and the divine dawn Goddess (Usha) were first recorded.

According to the Mahabharata, Karna, the son of Surya Bhagwan and ruler of Anga Desh (modern-day Bhagalpur and Munger in Bihar), was the first to establish the tradition of standing chest-deep in river waters at dawn and dusk to offer prayers to his father.

In the Ramayana, upon returning to Ayodhya after 14 years of exile and defeating Ravana, Lord Rama and Mata Sita observed a fast and performed Surya Yajna during Kartik Shukla Shashthi, establishing the coronation of the Ramrajya under solar blessing.

The Solar Science of Chhath

वैज्ञानिक दृष्टिकोण — सूर्य किरणों एवं जल का संगम

Chhath Puja is a masterclass in ancient bio-energetic science. During sunset and sunrise in the autumn transition month of Kartik, the angle of solar ultraviolet radiation is at its safest biological wavelength.

Standing in natural river water activates the body's natural conductivity. When solar rays pass through the water medium and the cow milk poured during Arghya, it prismatically refracts photons into seven spectral bands, stimulating the pineal and pituitary glands to boost immunity and mental tranquility.

👑 Legendary Chhath Geet Singers

Padma Bhushan Sharda Sinha (शारदा सिन्हा)
The Kokila of Bihar • Voice of Chhath Mahaparva
Renowned as the soul of Chhath Puja, Sharda Sinha immortalized timeless melodies such as "Ho Deenanath", "Kelwa Ke Paat Par", and "Pahile Pahil Hum Kayeni". Her golden voice has united millions of families across the globe during every Chhath festival for over five decades.

Trained in the authentic Bhojpuri folk traditions, Kalpana Patowary preserves raw, rural acoustic vibrancy and traditional vocal nuance in her soul-stirring Chhath Geet recordings.

About Chhath Geet

Chhath Geet is a digital sanctuary dedicated to the world's most ancient solar festival. Built with deep reverence for tradition, this app brings together 62 sacred audio songs, authentic 4-day ritual guidelines, live arghya panchang, and prasad recipes for devotees worldwide.
