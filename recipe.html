Thekua Recipe

The sacred wheat, jaggery and pure desi cow ghee prasad

🙏 Sacred Rules of Chhath Prasad

🔸
All ingredients must be pure and satvik (no onion, garlic, or eggs)
🔸
Use only pure desi cow ghee, sugarcane jaggery, and whole wheat flour
🔸
Prepare in a clean, sanctified space with pure devotion and thoughts

🍪 Batch Size

🌾 Ingredients (in Grams & Milliliters) (for 10 pieces)

🌾
Whole Wheat Flour
Coarsely ground pure whole wheat flour
250 g
🍯
Sugarcane Jaggery (Gur)
Pure unrefined sugarcane jaggery, finely chopped
150 g
🧈
Pure Desi Cow Ghee
Pure cow ghee for shortening & dough preparation
60 ml
🥥
Dry Grated Coconut
Finely shredded dry coconut flakes
30 g
🌰
Fennel Seeds (Saunf)
Aromatic thick green fennel seeds
10 g
🫚
Cardamom Powder
Freshly crushed green cardamom
3 g
💧
Pure Water
Clean water to dissolve jaggery into syrup
50 ml

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
🍯 Prepare Jaggery Syrup
Melt jaggery (150 g) in a pan with 50 ml water on low flame until dissolved into a light syrup. Strain to remove impurities.
💡 Tip: Do not overcook or boil excessively into hard caramel — keep syrup warm and light. 💡 सुझाव: चाशनी को बहुत गाढ़ा न करें — गुनगुनी और हल्की चाशनी रखें।
2
🌾 Prepare the Dough
In a large bowl, mix wheat flour (250 g), fennel seeds (10 g), cardamom powder (3 g), and grated coconut (30 g). Rub in 60 ml desi ghee evenly. Gradually add warm jaggery syrup and knead into a firm, stiff dough. Rest for 25–30 minutes.
💡 Tip: The dough must be firm and dense (tight), not soft like chapati dough. 💡 सुझाव: आटा रोटी के आटे जैसा नरम नहीं, बल्कि सख्त और टाइट गूंधें।
3
🍪 Shape the Thekua
Divide dough into equal lemon-sized portions (approx. 40 g each). Flatten into 1/2-inch thick oval or round discs. Press onto a traditional wooden thekua mold or fork to imprint designs.
💡 Tip: Traditional leaf and sun motifs help the thekua cook evenly from within. 💡 सुझाव: पारंपरिक सांचे या कांटे से डिजाइन बनाएं जिससे यह अंदर तक पके।
4
🔥 Deep Fry in Desi Ghee
Heat desi ghee in a deep kadhai on medium-low flame. Gently slide thekua discs in small batches. Fry patiently on low flame until both sides turn golden-brown (12–15 minutes).
💡 Tip: Maintaining low-medium heat is crucial for a crisp, flaky texture without burning. 💡 सुझाव: हमेशा धीमी आंच पर तलें जिससे यह अंदर तक खस्ता बने।
5
✨ Cool & Store
Drain on parchment paper and let cool completely on a wire rack. As they cool, they turn delightfully crisp and crunchy. Store in an airtight container for up to 3 weeks.
💡 Tip: Freshly fried thekuas are soft initially; they achieve signature crunch upon cooling. 💡 सुझाव: गरम ठेकुआ नरम रहता है, ठंडा होने के बाद ही यह कुरकुरा होता है।

✨ Pro Tips for Perfect Thekua

💡
Use coarsely ground whole wheat flour and pure cow desi ghee for authentic crunch.
असली स्वाद और खस्तापन के लिए मोटा पिसा आटा और शुद्ध गाय का घी उपयोग करें।
💡
Resting the dough for 25–30 minutes allows the gluten to relax and jaggery flavor to infuse deeply.
आटे को 25–30 मिनट ढककर रखने से गुड़ का स्वाद आटे में अच्छी तरह समा जाता है।
💡
Never rush frying on high heat — gentle low heat guarantees crispy golden outer crust and cooked core.
तेज आंच पर कभी न तलें — धीमी आंच पर तलने से ठेकुआ अंदर तक सुनहरा और खस्ता बनता है।
💡
Add a pinch of dry ginger powder (sonth) for enhanced digestive aroma and divine fragrance.
थोड़ी सी सोंठ (सूखा अदरक पाउडर) मिलाने से प्रसाद में दिव्य खुशबू और पाचक गुण आते हैं।
