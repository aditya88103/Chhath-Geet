Sacred Prasad

Thekua Recipe

ठेकुआ बनाने की विधि

Authentic wheat, jaggery & pure desi cow ghee recipe in metric grams (g) & milliliters (ml)

🙏 Sacred Rules of Chhath Prasad

🔸
All ingredients must be pure and satvik (no onion, garlic, or eggs)
सभी सामग्री शुद्ध और सात्विक होनी चाहिए
🔸
Use only pure desi cow ghee, jaggery, and whole wheat flour
केवल देसी घी, गुड़ और गेहूं का आटा उपयोग करें
🔸
Prepare in a clean, sanctified space with pure thoughts
स्वच्छ और पवित्र स्थान में शुद्ध विचारों से बनाएं

🍪 Batch Size (मात्रा)

🌾 Ingredients (for 10 pieces)

🌾
Whole Wheat Flour (गेहूं का आटा)
शुद्ध मोटा पिसा गेहूं का आटा
250 g
🍯
Sugarcane Jaggery / Gur (गुड़)
शुद्ध ईख का गुड़ (बारीक कटा)
150 g
🧈
Pure Desi Ghee (देसी गाय का घी - मोयन)
मोयन हेतु शुद्ध देसी घी
60 ml
🥥
Dry Grated Coconut (सूखा कद्दूकस नारियल / गढ़ी)
सूखा नारियल के बारीक लच्छे
30 g
🌰
Fennel Seeds / Saunf (सौंफ)
खुशबूदार मोटी हरी सौंफ
10 g
🫚
Green Cardamom Powder (हरी इलायची पाउडर)
ताज़ा कुटी छोटी इलायची
3 g
💧
Pure Water (पानी - चाशनी हेतु)
गुड़ घोलने हेतु स्वच्छ जल
50 ml

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
🍯 Prepare Jaggery Syrup
गुड़ की चाशनी बनाएं
Melt jaggery (150 g) in a pan with 50 ml water on low flame until dissolved into a one-string syrup. Strain to remove impurities.
💡 Tip: Do not overcook or boil excessively into hard caramel — keep syrup warm and light.
2
🌾 Prepare the Dough
आटा और मोयन तैयार करें
In a large bowl, mix wheat flour (250 g), fennel seeds (10 g), cardamom powder (3 g), and grated coconut (30 g). Rub in 60 ml desi ghee evenly. Gradually add warm jaggery syrup and knead into a firm, stiff dough. Rest for 25–30 minutes.
💡 Tip: The dough must be firm and dense (tight), not soft like chapati dough.
3
🍪 Shape the Thekua
ठेकुआ को आकार दें
Divide dough into equal lemon-sized portions (approx. 40 g each). Flatten into 1/2-inch thick oval or round discs. Press onto a traditional wooden thekua mold or fork to imprint designs.
💡 Tip: Traditional leaf and sun motifs help the thekua cook evenly from within.
4
🔥 Deep Fry in Desi Ghee
शुद्ध देसी घी में तलें
Heat desi ghee in a deep kadhai on medium-low flame. Gently slide thekua discs in small batches. Fry patiently on low flame until both sides turn golden-brown (12–15 minutes).
💡 Tip: Maintaining low-medium heat is crucial for a crisp, flaky texture without burning.
5
✨ Cool & Store
ठंडा करें एवं सुरक्षित रखें
Drain on parchment paper and let cool completely on a wire rack. As they cool, they turn delightfully crisp and crunchy. Store in an airtight container for up to 3 weeks.
💡 Tip: Freshly fried thekuas are soft initially; they achieve signature crunch upon cooling.

✨ Pro Tips for Perfect Thekua

💡
Use coarsely ground whole wheat flour (motā aatā) and pure cow desi ghee for authentic crunch.
💡
Resting the dough for 25–30 minutes allows the gluten to relax and jaggery flavor to infuse deeply.
💡
Never rush frying on high heat — gentle low heat guarantees crispy golden outer crust and cooked core.
💡
Add a pinch of dry ginger powder (sonth) for enhanced digestive aroma and divine fragrance.
